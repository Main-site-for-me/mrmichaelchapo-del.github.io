HOW TO BUILD A PLANET

Cosmic Engineering Tutorial — Level 5 Clearance

Step 1: Gather stardust. You need debris from old stars — iron, carbon, silicates. Mix until chunky.

Step 2: Spin it.

Rotation = gravity = clumping. The faster it spins, the faster it forms a sphere.

Step 3: Heat the core.

Melt the center until it becomes a glowing metal heart. This creates magnetic fields.

Step 4: Add oceans, land, or chaos.

Water? Lava? Ice? creatures? Your choice.

Step 5: Atmosphere time.

Wrap your planet in gases. Oxygen for life, methane for chaos, gases for vibes.

Step 6: Place it in orbit.

Find a star. Drop your planet into a stable path. Don’t let it crash.

Step 7: Name it.

Every planet needs a name. You can't just say: "I made a planet! It's called.
