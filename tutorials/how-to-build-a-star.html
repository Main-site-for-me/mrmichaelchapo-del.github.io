HOW TO BUILD A STAR

Cosmic Engineering Tutorial — Stellar Level Clearance

Step 1: Start with a giant cloud of gas.

Hydrogen + helium = the ingredients of every sun.

Step 2: Compress it.

Gravity pulls the cloud inward until it becomes a dense sphere.

Step 3: Heat the core.

As the gas collapses, temperature skyrockets. You need millions of degrees.

Step 4: Trigger fusion.

Hydrogen atoms fuse into helium. This releases insane energy — your star ignites.

Step 5: Stabilize it.

Gravity pulls inward, fusion pushes outward. Balance = a stable star.

Step 6: Choose its type.

Small red dwarf? Medium yellow sun? Massive blue giant? Your choice.

Step 7: Place it in your galaxy.

Drop your new star into a cosmic neighborhood. Planets will form around it.
